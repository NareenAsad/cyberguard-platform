# 🛡️ CyberGuard Platform

**CyberGuard** is an AI-driven threat intelligence and incident response platform designed to modernize cybersecurity operations through intelligent automation, contextual risk analysis, and real-time monitoring.

---

## 🎯 Overview

CyberGuard bridges critical gaps in modern cybersecurity by delivering:

* **Automated Threat Intelligence Aggregation**
  Collects and correlates data from trusted sources such as NVD, AlienVault OTX, ThreatFox, URLhaus, and AbuseIPDB.

* **AI-Powered Risk Assessment**
  Utilizes a multi-agent AI architecture to prioritize threats based on contextual and business impact.

* **Intelligent Incident Response**
  Automatically generates adaptive response playbooks using LLM-powered automation.

* **Real-Time Monitoring Dashboard**
  Interactive dashboards with live alerts, analytics, and threat visualizations.

* **Cost-Effective SOAR Alternative**
  Enterprise-grade automation at under 5% of the cost of traditional SOAR platforms.

---

## ✨ Core Features

* 🤖 **Multi-Agent AI Architecture**
  Dedicated agents for threat collection, vulnerability analysis, risk scoring, incident response, and reporting.

* 📊 **Live Security Dashboard**
  Real-time threat maps, prioritized alerts, and operational metrics.

* 🎯 **Contextual Risk Scoring**
  CVSS-based evaluation enhanced with business context and impact modeling.

* 📝 **AI-Generated Response Playbooks**
  Dynamic, threat-specific remediation workflows.

* 🔍 **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping**
  Automatic attack technique identification and threat classification.

* 📈 **Comprehensive Reporting**
  Executive summaries, detailed technical analysis, and compliance-ready documentation.
